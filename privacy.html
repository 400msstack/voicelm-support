Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

VoiceLM is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app connects to LLM servers on your local network and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Data Collection

VoiceLM does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers collected
  • No data sent to third-party servers

Voice & Speech Data

Voice input is processed using Apple's built-in Speech Recognition framework on-device or via Apple's servers depending on your iOS settings. VoiceLM does not store or access your voice recordings. Text-to-speech is handled by iOS built-in engines.

Local Network

VoiceLM communicates with LLM servers running on your local network (such as LM Studio or Ollama). All communication stays within your network. No conversation data is sent outside your device or local network.

Built-in AI Mode

When using Built-in AI mode (Apple Intelligence), all processing is performed on-device by Apple's Foundation Models framework. No data leaves your device in this mode.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the support page.
